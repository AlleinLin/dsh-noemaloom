`provenance.json` records each original path and SHA-256.

The original 26 role prompts, 16 complete framework texts, routes and gates are
method source data. They never grant capabilities, spawn agents, alter the host,
require an arbitrary coverage threshold, or attest a gate by themselves.
Nexus compiles them through its typed shape, ownership, permission, budget,
independence and evidence policies. The original controller, agent server,
fixed-PASS bridge, executable hooks and dependency tree are not imported.

The pure Nexus policies are newly implemented against specification chapter 12.
They retain the upstream precise identity, admission, actor evidence,
fingerprint, bounded adaptation and framework concepts with the modifications
recorded above.
